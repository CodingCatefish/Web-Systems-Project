About Pagemark

A neighborhood bookstore built around stories, conversation, and connection.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Pagemark started with one idea: a bookstore should feel like the heart of its neighborhood. We wanted a place where readers could discover new voices, revisit old favorites, and meet people who love stories.

Today, we host author talks, monthly reading circles, and collaborative learning events while maintaining a carefully selected catalog. Whether you are a long-time bibliophile or a casual weekend reader, there is always a spot for you here.
